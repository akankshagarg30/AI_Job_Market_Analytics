&#x20;**JobLens AI — AI-Powered Job Market Analytics Platform**

**---------------------------------------------------------------------------------**



**JobLens AI is an end-to-end job market analytics and intelligent job discovery platform that combines real-world job data, data analytics, resume analysis, skill extraction, and resume-to-job matching.**



**The platform helps users discover relevant job opportunities, analyze their resume against available jobs, identify skill gaps, and explore job market insights through an interactive dashboard.**







**## 🚀 Features--**





**- 🔎 Job Search**



&#x20; **- Search jobs by role, location, and skills**

&#x20; **- View job details and application links**

&#x20; **- Filter and explore relevant opportunities**



**- 📄 Resume Analysis**



&#x20; **- Upload and analyze a resume**

&#x20; **- Extract relevant skills from the resume**

&#x20; **- Compare resume skills against job requirements**

&#x20; **- Identify matched and missing skills**



**- 🎯 Resume–Job Matching**



&#x20; **- Calculate resume-to-job match scores**

&#x20; **- Categorize jobs based on match strength**

&#x20; **- Recommend relevant opportunities based on candidate skills**



**- 📊 Job Market Analytics**



&#x20; **- Analyze jobs by role**

&#x20; **- Analyze jobs by location**

&#x20; **- Identify frequently requested skills**

&#x20; **- Analyze resume-job match score distributions**

&#x20; **- Identify common missing skills**



**- 🏠 Featured Jobs**



&#x20; **- Display featured job opportunities on the home page**

&#x20; **- Provide direct application links**



**- 🔐 Privacy \& Contact**



&#x20; **- Dedicated privacy information section**

&#x20; **- Contact information for users**
